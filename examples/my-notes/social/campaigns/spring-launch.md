# Spring launch: Weekly Sales Snapshot

- **Goal:** 50 sign-ups for the free Weekly Sales Snapshot by 30 April.
- **Audience:** independent shop owners on LinkedIn; café and studio owners on X.
- **Platforms:** LinkedIn (3 posts a week), X (5 posts a week).
- **Dates:** 6 April to 30 April.
- **Key messages:**
  1. Your till already knows your best hour of the week. The Snapshot tells you every Monday.
  2. It takes two minutes to connect and it's free.
  3. Shops like yours found an extra hour of staffing they didn't need.
- **Link:** https://acme.example/snapshot
- **Success:** 50 sign-ups, and 10 replies from owners telling us what they'd want to know.

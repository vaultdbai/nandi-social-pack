# Our voice

**Who we are:** Acme Analytics, a 12-person company that helps small shops understand their sales data.

**Who we talk to:** owners and managers of independent shops, cafés and studios. Busy, practical, not technical.

**Tone:** warm, plain and specific. Short sentences. One useful idea per post. We sound like a helpful neighbour, not a brand.

**Use:** "you", real numbers from our own work, questions people can answer from experience.

**Avoid:** "game-changer", "revolutionary", "synergy", "leverage", hype, emoji walls, promises we can't keep.

**Hashtags:** at most three. #SmallBusiness #RetailData #ShopLocal

# Example notes for Social Manager

Copy the `social/` folder into My notes (Library › My notes, or the folder it shows) and edit it, or let Social Manager ask you and write its own.

- `social/voice.md`: a sample brand voice
- `social/campaigns/spring-launch.md`: a sample campaign
- `social/calendar.md`: its first week of posts
- `social/posts.md`: what went out (empty to start)

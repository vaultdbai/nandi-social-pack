# Social Manager

Runs your social media with you. It plans campaigns, writes posts in your voice, opens each post ready to go in a browser window you watch, and keeps track of what went out. **You press every Post button yourself.**

- **Prepares, you publish.** It types the post into LinkedIn's or X's own composer, then hands over: Nandi brings the window forward and your Inbox says "Your turn: review and post". Change anything, press Post, then tell Nandi: **Done**, **Skip** or **Changed it**.
- **Never automates engagement.** No likes, follows, connection requests, messages, reposts or comments on other people's posts. Its policy refuses those clicks everywhere, and refuses Post, Publish, Share, Send and Reply buttons on the social sites.
- **Never sees your password.** You sign in once, in its browser window. The session stays on your computer.
- **Keeps notes you can read.** Your voice, campaigns, calendar, what went out and weekly reports live in My notes under `social/`.

Needs: the **Browser** pilot (Library › Tools, with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge), and the **Web search** and **Filesystem** connectors with My notes shared read and write.

Sites it can open: LinkedIn and X (and the sites their pages load from: `*.licdn.com`, `*.twimg.com`). It uses your default model.
